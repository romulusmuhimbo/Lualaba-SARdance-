# Lualaba-SARdance-
Lualaba SARdance - NASA SPACE APP CHALLENGE 
Lualaba SARdance is a multidisciplinary team from the Democratic Republic of the Congo uniting a Geologist & Mining Engineer, Mining planner engineer, and a GIS & Cartography Specialist. Based in the heart of the Copperbelt, we leverage NASA SAR data (NISAR and OPERA) to monitor ground deformation, slope stability, and environmental dynamics namely across copper-cobalt mining landscapes in Lualaba.
